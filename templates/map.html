Groups are ordered by dependency: a group sits above the groups it imports, so imports flow down. Anything flowing up breaks that order and is labelled. Click a box to focus it and its neighbours. While one is focused, what it imports is drawn in blue and what imports it in grey; cycles, forbidden crossings and twins keep their colour. Double-click or Enter opens a group; inside, dashed stubs stand for the other groups and open them. Esc or a click on empty space clears.
